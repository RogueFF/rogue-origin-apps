Harvest Crew SMS Program

Rogue Family Farms · Rogue Origin · Southern Oregon · Program document, effective September 2026

This page describes the text-message program Rogue Family Farms runs with its own harvest barn supervisors. It is an internal operations tool. Recipients are employees. There is no marketing content and no customer messaging.

Who receives messages

Only barn supervisors employed by Rogue Family Farms during the hemp harvest. Two recipients in a typical season. Phone numbers are registered by the farm manager after the supervisor agrees in person to take part.

How recipients opt in (keyword)

A supervisor starts the day's messages by texting the keyword EMPEZAR (Spanish for "start") to the program number. Nothing is sent to a number until that keyword is received from it. The supervisor ends the day's messages by texting PARAR ("stop"). Messages also stop automatically each evening.

Message frequency and content

WhatDetail
PurposeHourly crew-count and production check-ins during harvest (people per role, racks hung, notes)
FrequencyOne prompt per hour while a supervisor has opted in, plus one reminder if there is no reply within 15 minutes, plus a confirmation after each reply. Typically 10 to 12 prompts per working day, none outside working hours, none after 8 PM Pacific
DirectionTwo-way. Supervisors reply with numbers and short notes and may ask simple questions about the day's totals
LanguageSpanish
SeasonHarvest only, roughly September to November
VolumeUnder 1,000 messages per month across all recipients

Opt out and help

Text PARAR or STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Text AYUDA or HELP for instructions. Text EMPEZAR or START to begin again. A supervisor can also ask the farm manager in person to remove their number.

Terms

Participation is voluntary and limited to current employees. Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's mobile plan. Messages are operational only; no promotions, offers, or third-party content are ever sent. Rogue Family Farms may end the program or remove a number at any time.

Privacy

The program stores the supervisor's name, phone number, barn assignment, and the content of messages exchanged. This information is used only to keep harvest production records and is retained as part of the farm's operating records. Phone numbers and message content are never sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for marketing. Messages are transmitted through Twilio, the farm's SMS provider, under Twilio's privacy terms.
